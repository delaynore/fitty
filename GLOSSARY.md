# Strength training

Fitty helps a lifter follow a training program and record their strength training.

## Language

**Training program**:
A plan that specifies what the lifter should do on each training day.
_Avoid_: Workout, routine

**Training day**:
A selectable plan in a training program that lists exercises. Each exercise may have a recommended number of sets and repetitions.
_Avoid_: Workout session

**Workout session**:
A single workout based on the training day the lifter chooses at the start.
_Avoid_: Training day, training program

**In-progress workout**:
A workout session the lifter has started and can still continue with its logged sets.

**Logged set**:
A set the lifter records in a workout session with the weight used and the number of repetitions completed.
_Avoid_: Planned set

**Workout history**:
The record of completed workout sessions and the sets logged in each. Exercises appear under the names they had at the time of the workout.
