# Strength training

Fitty helps a lifter follow a training program and record their strength training.

## Language

**Training program**:
A plan that specifies what the lifter should do on each training day.
_Avoid_: Workout, routine

**Training day**:
A selectable plan in a training program that lists exercises in the order the lifter intends to do them. Each exercise may have a target for that day.
_Avoid_: Workout session

**Exercise catalog**:
The collection of built-in exercises and exercises the lifter creates for reuse across training days.

**Exercise type**:
The kind of result the lifter records for an exercise, such as repetitions, a duration, or a weight and repetitions.

**Technical difficulty**:
How hard the exercise technique is to learn. The three levels are beginner, intermediate, and advanced.

**Primary muscle group**:
The muscle group an exercise mainly targets.

**Assisting muscle group**:
A muscle group that helps perform the movement while another group is the primary target.

**Recommended replacement**:
An exercise that closely matches the movement of the exercise the lifter wants to replace.

**Same-muscle alternative**:
An exercise that targets the same muscle group and can use a different movement or different equipment.

**Workout session**:
A single workout based on the training day the lifter chooses at the start.
_Avoid_: Training day, training program

**In-progress workout**:
A workout session the lifter has started and can still continue with its logged sets.

**Logged set**:
A record of what the lifter completed in one set, using the measures for that exercise type.
_Avoid_: Planned set

**Exercise timer**:
A timer the lifter starts to measure how long they perform a set.

**Rest timer**:
A countdown the lifter starts to time a rest between sets.

**Workout history**:
The record of completed workout sessions and the sets logged in each. Exercises appear under the names they had at the time of the workout.

**Fitty update**:
A newer Fitty release that can replace the installed app while preserving the lifter's training programs and workout records.
