# Fitty

Fitty is an offline strength training log for Android. Bring your own program to the gym and keep every set on your phone, even when the weight room has no signal.

**Keep your routine flexible.** Build training days around the exercises you actually do. If the bench is taken, jump ahead or swap in a similar movement for today. Your original plan is ready for next time.

**Pick up where you left off.** Fitty saves each set as you go. Close the app mid-workout and your session will be there when you return. Next week, you can look up exactly what you lifted.

**Keep your phone in your pocket.** For a timed hold, Fitty signals when you reach your target, even with the screen locked. It keeps counting until you stop, so those extra seconds make it into your log.
