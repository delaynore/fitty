# Fitty

Fitty is an offline strength training log for Android. Build your own training program, follow it at the gym, and review what you did afterward. Your programs and workout history stay on your phone.

## Install

On a phone running Android 8.0 or later, download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/delaynore/fitty/releases/latest) and open it to install Fitty. For future updates, open **Настройки** in Fitty and tap **Проверить обновления**.

## Track a workout

- Organize a program into training days. Pick exercises from the offline catalog or create your own.
- Choose a training day and log each set by weight and reps, reps alone, duration, or distance. Use the exercise timer for timed sets and the rest timer between sets.
- Reopen Fitty to resume an unfinished workout. After you finish, open History to review or correct your logged sets.

## Development

The Android app lives in `app/`. The [Android development guide](docs/android-development.md) covers local builds, tests, and signing a release APK.
